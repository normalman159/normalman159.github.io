My research focuses on building practical and reliable AI systems at the intersection of **Natural Language Processing**, **Generative AI**, **Retrieval-Augmented Generation**, and **Multi-Agent Systems**. I turn research ideas into usable systems, reproducible experiments, and project outcomes.

> **Research mission:** Build AI systems that are grounded in evidence, useful in practice, and transparent enough to evaluate and improve.

| Focus areas | Methods | Outputs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NLP, Generative AI, RAG, and Multi-Agent Systems | Retrieval, agent orchestration, LLM adaptation, and evaluation | Research papers, prototypes, open-source projects, and technical writing |

## Research directions

### Retrieval-Augmented Generation

I study how retrieval can improve the factuality, controllability, and domain adaptation of large language models.

**What I study:**

- Hybrid search and document grounding
- Query understanding and retrieval quality
- Evaluation for academic and institutional question-answering systems

**Related work:** [AskUIT AI Academic Assistant]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}), RAG survey notes, and academic support chatbots.

### Multi-Agent AI Systems

I explore how specialized agents can collaborate to solve information-seeking and reasoning tasks.

**What I study:**

- Task decomposition and agent routing
- Tool use, verification, and orchestration
- Reliable workflows for domain-specific AI assistants

**Related work:** Multi-agent architectures for academic assistance and domain-specific AI workflows.

### NLP and Generative AI Applications

I work on applied NLP systems that combine text understanding, generation, retrieval, and evaluation for real-world use cases.

**What I study:**

- LLM fine-tuning and LoRA-based adaptation
- Vietnamese and English NLP applications
- Practical evaluation of generated content and AI system behavior

**Related work:** [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) and technical notes on RAG, LLMs, and AI system design.
