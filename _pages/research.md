My research focuses on building practical and reliable AI systems at the intersection of **Natural Language Processing**, **Generative AI**, **Retrieval-Augmented Generation**, and **Multi-Agent Systems**. I am especially interested in turning research ideas into usable systems, reproducible experiments, and project outcomes.

## Research directions

### Retrieval-Augmented Generation

I study how retrieval can improve the factuality, controllability, and domain adaptation of large language models. My current interests include hybrid search, document grounding, query understanding, and evaluation for academic or institutional question-answering systems.

**Related work:** AskUIT AI Academic Assistant, RAG survey notes, academic support chatbots.

### Multi-Agent AI Systems

I explore how specialized agents can collaborate to solve information-seeking and reasoning tasks. This includes task decomposition, tool use, routing, verification, and orchestration patterns for AI assistants.

**Related work:** Multi-agent architecture for academic assistance and domain-specific AI workflows.

### NLP and Generative AI Applications

I work on applied NLP systems that combine text understanding, generation, retrieval, and evaluation. The long-term goal is to create AI tools that are useful, transparent, and easy to adapt to real-world domains.

**Related work:** LLM fine-tuning, LoRA-based adaptation, Vietnamese and English NLP applications.

## Selected project outcomes

- **AskUIT AI Academic Assistant** — a multi-agent RAG chatbot for academic support, recognized with an Encouragement Prize at SEApp 2025.
- **Competitive Programming Repository** — a long-running collection of optimized algorithmic solutions and reusable C++ templates.
- **Research writing and surveys** — technical writing on RAG, LLMs, and AI system design.
