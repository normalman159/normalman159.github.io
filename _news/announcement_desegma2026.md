**Paper accepted at DeSegMa-IT:** *Gradient Descenders at DeSegMa-IT: Leveraging Monolingual Transformer for LLM-Generated Text Detection and Boundary Identification* (with Bui Hong Son and Dang Van Thin).

The paper tackles detection of LLM-generated text by leveraging a monolingual Transformer, jointly addressing classification and boundary identification of machine-written spans. The approach was evaluated within the DeSegMa-IT shared task at the International Workshop on Evaluation of Natural Language and Speech Tools for Italian.
