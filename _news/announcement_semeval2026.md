**Paper accepted at SemEval-2026 Task 9:** *Gradient Descenders at SemEval-2026 Task 9: Data-Centric Counterfactual Augmentation for Multi-Label Hate Speech Detection* (with Dang Van Thin).

The work addresses multi-label hate speech detection by introducing a data-centric counterfactual augmentation strategy that generates targeted training perturbations. The approach improved robustness and generalization in the shared task evaluation, with findings presented at the 20th International Workshop on Semantic Evaluation (ACL).
