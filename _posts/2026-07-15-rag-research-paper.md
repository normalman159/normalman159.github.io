## Abstract

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines the strengths of parametric models with non-parametric memory to improve factual consistency and reduce hallucinations. This survey examines recent architectural innovations, training strategies, and evaluation methodologies in RAG systems published between 2024 and 2026.

## Key Findings

- **Hybrid retrieval**: Combining dense and sparse retrievers yields robust performance across domains.
- **Iterative refinement**: Multiple retrieval-generation cycles improve answer quality.
- **Tool integration**: RAG models that can call external tools (e.g., calculators, APIs) show enhanced reasoning capabilities.

## Conclusion

RAG remains a promising direction for building trustworthy and up-to-date AI assistants. Open challenges include efficient retrieval at scale and better attribution of generated text to source documents.
